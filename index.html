Profile

I am a cardiologist specializing in cardiac arrhtymias and catheter ablation. My clinical and research interests include atrial fibrillation, pulsed field ablation, and the development of innovative technologies in electrophysiology.

Activity

My reserach focuses on catheter ablation for cardiac arrhythmias, with a particular interest in pulsed filed ablation and clinical outcomes after atrial fibrillation ablation.
